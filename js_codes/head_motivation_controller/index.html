Head Motion Controller

📄 Legacy
How to Play
1
Press [UP] while looking up to collect training samples for the "up" gesture.
2
Press [STOP] while looking straight ahead to collect "neutral" samples.
3
Repeat steps 1–2 until the network recognizes your head motion reliably.
4
Start the game and control it with your head!
